# Assistant layer research: best-practice scorecard

Written 2026-09-16 for the layer above retrieval in the Burger's Bar staff assistant: the system prompt and routing policy, the bounded tool loop over Gemini 3.1 Pro through OpenRouter, the six read-only app tools, the broker's Google-backed web search, the source chips, the answer log and the evaluation harness. The retrieval pipeline (chunking, embeddings, hybrid search) is out of scope; it was researched separately in `docs/rag-research`.

## Method

The research ran in two passes. Both drew on primary sources (vendor docs, papers, engineering blogs, laws and regulators' pages from the last 18 months) fetched on 2026-09-16, and both audited read-only against the worktree `.claude/worktrees/assistant-web` on branch `feat/assistant-web-search` at dbf2f44 (#385).

- Pass one, four lenses: (1) the decision layer, when to search or call a tool versus answer directly; (2) grounding and citing; (3) agent loop mechanics and safety; (4) UX, evaluation and cost. Four audit dimensions: system prompt and routing policy; loop mechanics and safety; provenance and UX; evaluation and observability. 39 findings raised, 10 adversarially verified: 10 confirmed (5 at a reduced severity), 0 refuted; the other 29 recorded unverified.
- Pass two, nine lenses covering what pass one left out, the last three of them added by a coverage critic: (5) conversation design, persona and memory; (6) permission-aware data access and tool design for app data; (7) privacy, compliance and content guardrails for an employee assistant in Israel; (8) feedback, knowledge hygiene and continuous improvement; (9) delivery engineering, streaming, limits and resilience; (10) structured company knowledge and canonical facts; (11) input modalities, photo, file and voice; (12) from answers to actions, write tools with confirmation and proactive briefs; (13) Hebrew-native quality and language policy. Three audit dimensions: conversation, persona, formatting and memory (C); permissions, privacy, compliance and guardrails (D); feedback, knowledge hygiene, delivery and operations (K). 28 findings raised, 10 verified: 10 confirmed (7 at a reduced severity), 0 refuted; the other 18 recorded unverified.
- Evidence came from the code, from tsx and node experiments that drove the pure functions and the real tool factory against a fake or stubbed LLM client (no paid call, no network), and from the vitest suites (tool-loop, llm-tools, llm-config, assistant-prompt: 55 of 55 green; assistant-tools: 17 green; document-metadata: 18 green). The retrieval pipeline (chunking, embeddings, hybrid search) is out of scope; it was researched separately in `docs/rag-research`.
- Reading the tables. Consensus level: consensus (every leader does it), contested (leaders differ or the owner has ruled), emerging (research or the newest vendor features, not yet settled). Status: BUILT, PARTIAL, MISSING, NOT_APPLICABLE. Code references are file:line under `apps/api/src/assistant/` unless another path is given, in the worktree above. Research references are keys such as [A1] into the Sources list at the end: pass one's keys are unchanged, and pass two's use new prefixes ([C] conversation, [S] security and permissions, [P] privacy and law, [K] knowledge and operations, [D] delivery, [B] company knowledge and chains, [V] input modalities, [W] write actions, [H] Hebrew). Finding ids such as P1, LOOP-3, C1, D3 or K2 point into `02-implementation-audit.md`.

## Top gaps

One merged list across all thirteen lenses, in order of how much the MISSING or PARTIAL status hurts, given the owner's policy (company sources first, then the web, then general knowledge; never an invented fact; sources shown as chips) and the client conversation ahead.

1. **The evaluation measures a prompt the product no longer sends, and no eval item knows who is asking** (Lens 4 rows 5, 4 and 6; Lens 1 row 14; Lens 6 row 8; Lens 8 row 10; MISSING). Both harnesses drive buildGuardrailSystemPrompt with no tools and no web (assistant-eval.ts:408, 491, 578), no golden item carries a route, a tool expectation or a role (verified: zero "role" keys across all four eval files), retrieval runs as admin while the prompt is built as employee, no answer records which prompt produced it, and CI never runs eval or probe. A green run today would mislead, and every other gap on this list is unmeasurable until it is fixed. Findings EO-2, EO-3, P10, EO-6, D7.
2. **Entitlement is enforced well inside the SQL and governed nowhere else** (Lens 6 rows 1, 5, 6, 9, 11; MISSING and PARTIAL). The read-side design is right, but the confidential tier includes the branch-bound `admin` role so a Talpiot admin can ask for the Eilat lease; every role is offered every tool with the WhatsApp gate hard-coded outside the Access page; people_directory ships every visible colleague's work email on every call and returns people who have left as current staff; and no threat model maps tool to scope predicate to capability key, so the client CEO's written department-permission requirement has no evidence behind it. Findings D1, D2, D6, D7.
3. **What we have published about privacy is not what the code does** (Lens 7 rows 1, 2, 3; Lens 6 row 14; MISSING). The privacy page tells staff in both languages that the search engine receives "not your question or any company document" while nothing constrains what the model writes into a Google query and nothing can even detect it; the WhatsApp ingestion, its 46 groups of named-person content and the answer log are absent from that page entirely; the request carries no zero-data-retention or data-collection flag; and the assistant makes no AI disclosure anywhere, with the policy route deliberately unlinked until two placeholders are filled. Findings D3, D4, D5, D10, F9.
4. **Freshness is a description, not an instruction, and inside our own corpus nothing carries a date or an owner** (Lens 1 rows 2 and 6; Lens 2 row 5; Lens 8 row 6; Lens 10 rows 8 and 9; PARTIAL). Nothing tells Gemini 3.1 Pro that its knowledge ends around January 2025, nothing says "run the web search before answering", nothing checks a false premise, and no "as of" form exists; separately, knowledge_docs has no owner, verified_at or deprecated_at and neither stored date reaches the grounding block, so a superseded price list ranks first and answers as confidently as the current one. Findings P1, K1.
5. **A general-knowledge answer carries no label** (Lens 1 row 3; Lens 4 row 3; MISSING). No prompt clause asks the model to declare tier 3, no server line is appended when the trace and citations are both empty, and the `general` chip that ADR-0028 and the shared schema both describe has no emitter, while the UI copy promises a source under every answer. Findings F1, P6.
6. **Web citations earned on a tool-calling round are dropped** (Lens 1 row 11; Lens 2 rows 1 and 12; PARTIAL). tool-loop.ts:209 keeps only the final round's citations, so a mixed document-plus-web answer can state the web fact with no chip and log the paid search as empty or not at all. Found independently by three auditors and verified against the real loop. Findings P2, LOOP-1, EO-1, F2.
7. **The loop's budget is invisible to the model and the reader, and the two-search cap does not bind on the live engine** (Lens 1 row 7; Lens 3 row 2; Lens 4 row 11; MISSING and PARTIAL). The prompt states no budget or stop rule and the withheld final call carries no notice; capped and rounds reach neither the reply nor the log; max_uses and max_results are ignored by Google native and the loop's own count depends on a figure the engine has not reported, so up to eight paid searches per answer are possible where the owner asked for two. Findings P3, P5, LOOP-2, LOOP-7.
8. **Nothing closes the loop: no feedback, no outcome, no reader** (Lens 8 rows 1, 2, 3, 4, 5; MISSING). A person who gets a wrong answer cannot say so, an honest "I did not find it" is logged as a success because the status enum has only answered and unavailable, nobody is ever told what the corpus is missing, and assistant_answer_log has no reader anywhere in apps/api/src although the row already carries the role, the tools in call order and the retrieved document ids. Findings K3, K4, EO-7.
9. **The delivery path has no stream, no idempotency, no limit, no fallback, no drain and no health signal** (Lens 4 row 1; Lens 9 rows 1, 2, 3, 9, 10, 11; MISSING). A 10 to 65 second loop shows one generic spinner with the bilingual tool labels unused; a retry after a dropped connection buys the same answer twice and leaves a duplicate exchange; nothing rate-limits the paid route; a Google-side error turns every question into a 503 until someone edits env and redeploys, with no alert; every deploy kills answers in flight against a 10-second grace; and /health reads green straight through an assistant outage. Findings F3, K2, K5, K6, K7, K8.
10. **Cost, searches, rounds and cap hits are not logged** (Lens 4 rows 10 and 12; Lens 9 rows 12 and 13; PARTIAL). WireCompletion ignores usage.cost, the cached and reasoning token splits and the served model; the log has no cost, rounds, capped or web_searches column; failed loops log null tokens; no session_id is sent for sticky routing and no cached_tokens is parsed, so even the within-answer cache is unmeasurable; the only alert is a $5 balance floor polled hourly. ADR-0028's cost figures cannot be checked from production, and the Hebrew token premium behind them is still an estimate (measured on the repo's own parallel pairs at 1.22x under o200k_base and 2.94x under cl100k_base). Findings EO-4, LOOP-9, EO-5.
11. **The answer's shape and direction break in the app's primary language** (Lens 5 rows 3, 4, 6; Lens 10 row 3; Lens 13 rows 1 and 3; MISSING and PARTIAL). dir="auto" sits on the whole bubble so one Latin first character flips a whole Hebrew answer LTR; the prompt permits tables, links and fences the renderer cannot draw; the raw role enum reaches the model so a Hebrew reply naming a role has to coin one; nothing governs gendered second-person address, which WinoMT's Hebrew numbers put at 46 to 54 percent accuracy across four commercial systems; and a free-text branch query misses every transliteration, prefix and word form. Findings C1, C2, C3, C6, C7.
12. **Model parameters contradict the vendor, and the production model id is a preview with a retirement clock** (Lens 3 rows 10 and 11; Lens 8 row 11; Lens 9 row 4; MISSING and PARTIAL). ANSWER_TEMPERATURE 0.2 rides every Gemini 3.1 Pro call against Google's explicit 1.0 recommendation and a 256-token reasoning cap is sent where Google maps budgets to a level with no precise control, with reasoning_tokens never read; meanwhile google/gemini-3.1-pro-preview is pinned as a literal in render.yaml and .env.prod.example, Google gives preview models "at least 2 weeks notice", its deprecations page already records gemini-3-pro-preview shut down on 2026-03-09, and no cutover plan, fallback model or eval gate on a model swap exists. Findings P9, LOOP-6, K2.

Adjacent to gaps 3, 5 and 6: web chips carry no date and hide the host, and the annotation's snippet and indices are thrown away (Lens 2 rows 1, 5 and 13; finding F4), which blocks the "as of" form, inline anchoring and any post-answer check. Adjacent to gaps 2 and 8: the Knowledge tab's read model carries no usage, quality or verification number, so the people who own the corpus cannot see which of their documents the assistant is failing on.
