# Assistant layer research: roadmap

Written 2026-09-16, updated after the second research pass. Ordered work list from `01-best-practices.md` (the scorecard, thirteen lenses) and `02-implementation-audit.md` (the findings). Finding ids (P, LOOP, F, EO from pass one; C, D, K from pass two) point into 02; "01, Lens n row m" points at a scorecard row with no finding of its own.

## For the owner, in six lines

Today the assistant does the hard part right: it looks in the company's documents and the app before the web, every source chip under an answer comes from something the server actually ran so a made-up source cannot appear, each person's reach is decided inside the database query from the session rather than in the prompt, the six tools are read-only with no write path, the loop is capped in rounds, time and paid searches, and no question or answer text is kept in the operational log.
Demo risk one, what it says: a public fact that changes, such as the VAT rate or the minimum wage, can still come from the model's memory, which stops around January 2025, with no search and no warning; an answer given from general knowledge looks exactly like a greeting or a miss; and inside our own corpus no document carries a date, an owner or a verified state, so a 2024 price list answers as confidently as this year's and in fact ranks above it.
Demo risk two, who sees what: a branch admin can ask for any other branch's lease and franchise terms, the people list returns colleagues who have left as current staff with their work email, every role is offered every tool including the WhatsApp summaries, and no test anywhere asks the same question as two different people, so the client CEO's written department-permission requirement has no evidence behind it.
Demo risk three, what we have published: the privacy page tells staff in both languages that the search engine never receives their question or a company document, and nothing in the code enforces or can even detect that; the WhatsApp ingestion of 46 groups and the answer log are not on that page at all; and on the delivery side a 10 to 65 second answer sits behind one spinner, a retry after a dropped phone pays for the same answer twice, and if Google has a bad hour every question returns an error with no fallback and no alert.
The next two weeks should ship one "assistant hardening" change in two batches: the knowledge-cutoff line and the order to search before answering a changing fact, the general-knowledge label, web chips that survive every round, a stated lookup budget with a visible "partial answer" notice, cost and search counts written to the log, plus the who-sees-what batch, the confidential-tier fix, an account status on people rows, the zero-data-retention flags and the rule that nothing from a tool result may ride into a web query.
In the same two weeks the evaluation must be rewritten to drive the real assistant (tools, web search, the live prompt) with route labels, an Israeli freshness set and a cross-role leakage suite, so that a stale VAT answer or a branch admin reading another branch's lease fails a test on our machine instead of failing in front of the client; and fifteen decisions listed in group (e) below need the owner or head office, of which two, the business name and contact email on the privacy page, block both store listings today.

## Sizes and sequencing

S is under half a day, M is one to three days, L is a week-class slice. PR3 (the public website mirrored into the knowledge base) and PR4 (the evaluation rewrite) are the two slices ADR-0028 already schedules (docs/adr/0028-assistant-tool-loop-sources-first.md:93-96); the hardening PR is new and can start at once because it touches neither. Group (e) is not engineering work at all: it is the decisions and content the owner or head office has to supply, several of which gate items in the other groups. The "next two weeks" of the summary means hardening batches 1 and 2 plus PR4 items 1 to 4 and 11.
